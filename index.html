About Metropole

Cape Town's Leading
Student Accommodation
Network

Metropole has been providing premium student accommodation across Cape Town for over a decade. From Rosebank to Sea Point, Green Point to the City Bowl — we place students at the heart of the city, close to campus, and surrounded by everything they need to thrive.

Every Metropole property is fully managed, accredited, and designed with the modern student in mind. Whether you're in your first year or finishing your degree, there's a Metropole that's right for you.

High-Speed Wi-Fi

Uncapped fibre in every room. Study, stream, and connect without limits.

24-Hour Security

Biometric access, CCTV, armed response, and panic buttons throughout.

Fully Furnished

Double bed, study desk, wardrobe — move in with just your suitcase.

Managed & Accredited

Professional on-site management and full accreditation at every property.

A decade of excellence.
